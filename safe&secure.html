Safety Tips for Passengers

Follow these simple tips to ensure a smooth and secure journey

Book Only Through Official Channels

Always book tickets through IRCTC official website, authorised agents, or the official RAIL platform. Avoid unauthorised third-party sites.

Never Share Your Password or OTP

Indian Railways or RAIL will never ask for your password, OTP, or bank details over phone, email, or SMS. Report suspicious activity immediately.

Keep Your Luggage Secure

Use TSA-approved locks for your luggage. Never leave your belongings unattended, and keep valuables with you at all times.

Carry Valid ID Proof

Always carry a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID) during your journey. It's mandatory for all passengers.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice any suspicious person, object, or activity, immediately inform the RPF, GRP, or call the helpline 139. Do not touch any unattended object.

Keep Emergency Contacts Handy

Save the Railway Helpline (139), Women Helpline (182), and Medical Helpline (138) on your phone before your journey.

Use Secure Wi-Fi Networks

Avoid using public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks for booking tickets or accessing banking apps. Use mobile data or VPN when possible.

Verify Your PNR Before Boarding

Always check your PNR status and coach/seat details before boarding. Download the official app to get real-time updates.
