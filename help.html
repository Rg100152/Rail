❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a train ticket?
You can book a train ticket by going to the Search page, entering your journey details (from, to, date, class), and clicking "Search Trains". Select your preferred train, enter passenger details, and proceed to payment.
How can I check my PNR status?
Go to the PNR Status page (available in the quick links) or visit Rail Dishti page. Enter your 10-digit PNR number and click "Check Status" to get real-time updates on your booking confirmation and waiting list status.
What should I do if my payment fails?
If your payment fails, please check with your bank if the amount has been deducted. If not, you can try booking again. If the amount is deducted but booking is not confirmed, contact our support team at 139 or care@rail.co.in with your transaction details.
Can I cancel my ticket and get a refund?
Yes, you can cancel your ticket from the My Bookings section. Refund policy depends on the time of cancellation. Please check the cancellation policy for details. Cancellation charges may apply.
How do I order food on the train?
You can order food from the Food Booking page. Select your train, choose your meal, and place the order. Food will be delivered to your seat during the journey. Pre-ordering at least 2 hours before departure is recommended.
How can I recover my account if I forget my password?
Go to the Forgot Password or Account Recovery page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a reset link. Follow the instructions to create a new password and regain access to your account.
